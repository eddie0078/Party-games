Game Rules & Guide

👥 Player & Gender Awareness

When adding players, set their name and gender (Male or Female). The game automatically customizes pronouns and couples/flirty challenges so prompts like "kiss {target_opposite} on the cheek" or "sit on {target}'s lap" adapt intelligently!

🎭 Truth or Dare (240+ Questions)

  • 🟢 Classic: Safe, fun, friendly icebreakers for any group.
  • 🟡 Party: Funny, wild, goofy dares and mildly embarrassing truths.
  • 🔴 Spicy: Romantic, flirty, crush and couple challenges.
  • 🟣 Extreme: Savage, bold, unfiltered challenges.
  • Scoring: Complete a challenge for +10 points. Chicken out for -5 points.

💬 Get to Know You (225+ Questions)

Explore 5 categories: Icebreaker, Deep & Soulful, Love & Dating, Hypotheticals, and Hot Takes. Play in Hot Seat mode (directed to one player) or All Room mode where everyone takes turns answering.

🍾 Spin the Bottle

Gather your friends and tap the bottle to spin with realistic physics. The selected player immediately steps up for a Truth, Dare, or Question!
